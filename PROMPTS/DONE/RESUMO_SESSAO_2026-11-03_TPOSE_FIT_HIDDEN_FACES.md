## Built
- **`tripo_fit_arm_t.py`** — the arm edits, made in the T file: the shirt's sleeve underside lowered and pushed toward the ribs, clamped on the torso's own wall (an unclamped push put the cap's front through the chest and left shards); the arms lowered as ONE field by the rig's arm weight (never by x position: that left the shoulder behind and tore the cap off). Per figure: female DROP 0.01 m, smoothed field R 0.08; male DROP 0.015 m, DX 0.02, R 0 (the shirt and hands by their own weight, the coat following the shirt's nearest vertices: a smoothed field left knobs on the male's shoulder caps).
- **`tripo_fit_from_tpose.py`** — the EXACT way back from a T file: p_A = M_v^-1 p_T per vertex (the armature modifier's blended matrix, the weights the T file holds); `FT_CHECK=1` proves it, `FT_KEY=<name>` writes the edit into a shape key instead of the rest.
- **`tripo_shirt_tidy.py`** — relaxes the shirt where the T stretched it: WELDS the copies of a vertex by their A position (the mesh splits a vertex at every UV / normal seam and each copy moved alone: a plain Laplacian tore the shirt), measures per place the largest edge stretch T / A, masks from that, Taubin on the places. Options: `TT_RING` (a band around each arm's axis: the torso's armhole rim), `TT_GEO` (neighbours by distance: merges the folded sheets), `TT_SEW` (closes the small boundary loops, the shirt's seams), `TT_SPHERE` (smooths a ball at any weight).
- **`tripo_sleeve_root_close.py`** — closes a sleeve's open root like a dome (the male's right side; REJECTED by the Director, it "sucked" the side in). Kept as an option.
- **`tripo_hidden_study.py`**, **`tripo_hidden_delete_test.py`**, **`tripo_hidden_apply.py`** — see D98.
- **`tripo_hair_clip.py`** — keeps the hair out of the coat (cut mode, push mode); neither adopted.

## Found / measured
- **The way back is exact.** An unedited T file returns the A file's positions to 2·10^-7 m on all ten meshes of both figures. In the video, the posed shirt and hands at the T equal the edited T file to 0.0000 m; the coat differs up to 4-5 cm on ~176 vertices per figure (the T bake moves the coat's chest weight to Spine2, the A weights do not: known).
- **A T-pose edit is an edit of the A.** Lowering the arms 1.5 cm in the T is a 1.5 cm shift INWARD in the A (the arm turns 52-69 degrees between them): the arms came back narrower by 1.3 cm (female) and 2.5 cm (male) on average. The cure is not to write it into the A: the `TFix` key carries it at value 1 and the A is identical to the original at value 0 (0 m).
- **A hand-made T is not the A's topology.** The Director's files merged 17 k vertices by distance and were hand-edited: the vertex order and count no longer match the A or the `TFix` files, so `from_tpose` cannot read them as they are.
- **The dark crater under a raised arm is OUTSIDE surface**, not the inside of the shirt (the in/out test: grey nearly everywhere, red only in slivers). The real defects were: the shirt's two armpit seams (the original female shirt has only 20 open edges, two loops of 6 at the armpits: 3-4 cm gaps in the T, nearly shut in the A), two folded sheets around the armhole rim, and the male's open sleeve roots (two loops per side, arm weight 0.9-1).
- **The hidden-face study** (`AGENT_<FIG>.glb`, poses A, T and four frames of each clip `dive`, `heart_pose`; the board's four facings x pitches 20/30/45; every game material is double sided, so only OCCLUSION hides a face): faces never seen, with only the KEPT pieces in the scene (SAFE): male shirt 30.3 %, trousers 10.7 %, hands 0.1 %; female shirt 7.8 %, trousers 1.2 %, hands 3.9 %; seen only under today's replaceable pieces (COND): male shirt +2.7 %, trousers +10.6 %; female +1.5 %, +5.6 %. Where: the male shirt's hem tucked into the trousers, the collar's inside, the cuffs' inside; the trousers' waist and hems (COND).
- **The pixel gate was earned and the first deletion failed it:** two untouched runs differ by 0 pixels; deleting every SAFE face changed 13 885 (male) and 7 200 (female) pixels over 64 images, slivers along lines where surfaces cross; keeping every hidden face that touches a seen one (one ring) brings it to 5 and 0. In the fit files, after the ring: female 1 pixel in 17.9 M, male 475 (23 in the A, ~450 at the T's armpits). Face counts after the ring: male 1 419 faces (shirt 4 586 -> 3 471, trousers 3 600 -> 3 296), female 229.
- **Planar dissolve does not pay:** angle 3-6 degrees removes 1-5 % of the shirt's faces and changes 0.1-0.2 % of the pixels (nearly all in the T); the mesh is not flat there. Rejected.
- **Hair through the collar:** 423 hair vertices lie inside the coat's collar (x +-0.05, y 0.015-0.061, z 0.871-0.907); the coat is not watertight (229 open edges) so "inside" is a vote of 8 rays. Push mode crumples the tips into flakes on the collar; cut mode removes 1 140 + 45 faces. Neither accepted.
- **Traps (new):** (1) a glTF's embedded action overrides any pose you set (`animation_data_clear()` first; the first study ran with the figure in `dive`); (2) a shirt mesh has duplicated vertices at every seam: smooth by PLACE (weld by the A position), not by copy; (3) a smoothed per-mesh weight field tears the coat from the shirt at the shoulder: one field for all; (4) selection by x position cuts the shoulder in half: select by rig weight; (5) Blender's own Python has no PIL: save arrays and plot outside; (6) `.blend1` is the state before the LAST save only (it helped undo one step, never two); (7) the glTF import keeps the face order: shirt / trousers of the GLB and the fit files share it (trousers identical, shirt within 5 mm), which is what makes the study's indices valid on the fit files.
