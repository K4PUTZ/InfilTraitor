## 0b. Director's rulings (2026-10-11)

- **R-D1 — THE LIGHT ABOVE A ROOF (answers Q1).** *"Vamos ter luz externa em alguns cenários mais abertos (sol, lua, nublado, ambiente). Mas em geral os cenários serão internos e as luzes vão afetar zonas internas menores. Seria basicamente o conceito de um galpão com cubículos/aposentos, que vão ter sua luz própria, mas que também podem ser influenciados pela luz maior quando o telhado quebra."* So the light a hole lets in is **whatever light is ABOVE that roof**: the map's outdoor light (one of sun / moon / overcast / ambient, open scenes only) or, in the common case, the enclosing hall's own light over a cubicle's roof. A room keeps its own lamps; breaking its roof ADDS the larger light through the hole.
- **R-D2 — SHOTS REACH ROOFS (answers Q3).** *"Os tiros por natureza são horizontais atualmente […] Nós vamos querer porém ter o gameplay onde o agente deliberadamente atira no telhado para causar alguma mudança no cenário. […] na prática vai ser o mesmo comportamento do vidro vertical, mas com as consequências, chuva, pó, caindo pra baixo."* A shot aimed at a roof behaves as a shot on a vertical pane (the hole, the craze, the support rule, G-D49's re-damage), with the consequences falling DOWN (rain, dust). The aiming gesture (how the player targets a roof) is designed with the interface, not here; the engine takes a shot whose target is a roof cell.
- **R-D3 — AN INTACT GLASS ROOF IS TRANSLUCENT (answers Q2).** *"O telhado de vidro intacto tem opacidade, de maneira que a luz passa, porém restrita. Quando o vidro é destruído ela fica acentuada (full)."* An intact glass roof lets a RESTRICTED share of the light above through (a per-material transmission factor); a broken cell lets it through in full.

- **R-D5 — A SKYLIGHT IS A HORIZONTAL WINDOW, BUILT LIKE THE WALL'S PANE.** *"Pela forma que a gente construiu as paredes, são duas camadas de voxels, uma em cada GU que se encontram. E os vidros ocupam apenas metade da espessura, em uma ou outra GU […] criando uma película de vidro mais fina entre duas paredes, e deixando um gap de meia espessura entre elas. Precisamos fazer o mesmo com o telhado. Ou seja a claraboia, que pode eventualmente ser o teto todo (depois a gente faz designs mais específicos), vai ser uma janela na horizontal, com as slabs em duas camadas paralelas ao redor, e o vidro ocupando o centro do bloco, na camada mais pra baixo, deixando o gap na camada de cima."* The roof is two parallel layers (its two levels, `ROOF_LEVEL_COUNT`); the skylight is the roof's centre with the glass in the LOWER layer only and the upper layer EMPTY (a half-thickness recess seen from above, flush with the ceiling from below), the frame around it in both layers. A skylight may be the whole roof. ⚠️ The ROOF_LAB room A built at step 1 has glass in BOTH layers (the Director's observation): it is rebuilt to this form.
- **R-D6 — A BLAST ON A SKYLIGHT.** *"Quando a granada explode, os voxels de vidro voam pra cima e pros lados, seguindo a força que já usamos no vidro vertical, e depois caem no chão, ou sobre a lateral do cubículo. A chuva e o pó acompanham, seguindo o mesmo mecanismo da janela vertical, adaptando a orientação. No final a luz é atualizada."* The broken glass leaves along the shockwave impulse the vertical pane already uses (`GlassFall`'s `{from, strength, lift}`, G4-4), UP and out from a blast below, then falls under gravity onto whatever is under it: the floor, the frame, a wall's top (the landing index already counts CEILING slabs as surfaces). Rain and dust are the vertical pane's, oriented to a horizontal face. The light updates at the end (the consequence light, R-D4).
- **R-D7 — THE OTHER ROOF MATERIALS** (concrete, plywood / *madeirite*, cardboard, fabric, wood) behave alike, holding / breaking more or less by material (§3).
- **R-D8 — SHOTS, ON A ROOF AND ON A WALL.** *"Tiros deixam as mesmas marcas, decals, etc. e podem abrir buracos, e fazer os voxels voarem também pra cima (vamos lembrar de fazer isso com os tiros laterais também, quando quebrarem voxels a gente faz a mesma animação do vidro sem sustentação, com o crossfade se necessário, fazendo o(s) voxel(eis) voar(em) até o chão)."* A shot into a roof leaves the wall's marks and decals, can open a hole, and the voxels it breaks FLY (up, along the shot) and fall. **And a lateral shot on a wall does the same**: the voxels any shot breaks leave as flying voxels (`GlassVoxelFall3D`'s motion and crossfade, generalised to every material) instead of vanishing. Stage RF-S below.
- **R-D9 — ONE RUBBLE MACHINE (2026-10-12, Director chose the recommended option).** The opaque voxels a shot breaks (wall or roof) fly and pile through `PropFragmentSim` / `PropFragments3D`, extended with a DIRECTIONAL impulse (along the shot) and an uncharred option; the pile is the Tier 4 pile (stacks, slumps, base-coord record, checkpoint restore). Glass keeps `GlassVoxelFall3D` (it shatters on landing). This supersedes R-D8's "`GlassVoxelFall3D` generalised to every material" for opaque voxels: one pile in the game, not two.
- **R-D10 — A ROOF SHOT IS A STRAIGHT RAY (2026-10-12, Director chose the recommended option).** From the muzzle to the target roof cell's centre in 3D; it stops at the first solid voxel it crosses (a wall on the way takes the round); a roof hit lands on the lower layer's underside; the broken voxels fly up along the ray and fall.
- **R-D11 — CLOTH IS A MATERIAL, FINISHED BEFORE IT IS BURNT (2026-10-16, Director).** *"Está ficando bom, acho que vai funcionar."* A cloth roof is not a game roof (the game will rarely have one): it exists so that **any cloth in the future behaves right**. Rulings: (a) a cloth is a sheet with **no voxel thickness**, drawn as one plane over its index voxels (v0.30), but with a **minimum visible thickness** so it never reads as paper; (b) the **gap at the supports** is a defect, and the cheap fix is to let the cloth **STRETCH** to the support (a tether of limited extension); **tearing** (a ragged edge of threads and a spare of cloth on the slab / wall) is legitimate and richer but dearer, so it comes later; (c) the **flaps** on the hole's rim are good, but **smaller, longer and more of them, hanging from the hole's edges**; (d) a **fallen cloth is scenery** (it never reacts to a blast again, only soot); (e) **fire and soot come AFTER the cloth's behaviour is closed** ("é basicamente aplicar vfx e shader"): the squares are the soot ladder's 1/8 GU resolution and the fire is not synchronised with the moving cloth (the embers are drawn by the plane, not by the surface).
- **R-D12 — WHAT HAS NO SUPPORT FALLS (2026-10-16, Director; the four open points ruled the same day).** *"Todo elemento do mundo sem sustentação cai, independente da natureza ou do material (dá pra deixar uma chave pra ligar/desligar a gravidade, e controlar a intensidade)."* A structural rule of the whole world, not of roofs: after ANY destruction, every standing claim with no path of standing claims to the ground falls. Rulings: (1) **support = full glue for now**: contact on any face holds, whatever the material (a per-material sideways glue is a later refinement); (2) the fall is a **short progressive cascade**, each piece starting a little after the neighbour that held it; (3) **scope: props and posts first**, then roofs and glass folded into the same service, walls last; (4) **persistence**: what falls is destroyed and base-recorded, its pile is recorded like any rubble pile, a checkpoint restores it. **Two switches**: `STRUCT_GRAVITY` (on / off, `DevFlags`) and `STRUCT_GRAVITY_SCALE` (the fall's gravity and so its duration, default 1.0). Triggered by the same moment as the glass collapse (the end of a destruction commit), run off the main thread like `GlassSupport`.

## 0c. R-D4 — the light mechanism (APPROVED 2026-10-11: "Aprova, aceita a mudança"; BUILT the same day, see §5)

Found while building step 1: **the light is 2D.** `VoxelLightField._lamp_term()` lights a (GU, level) by distance from each lamp (plus `ShadowProjector`'s HORIZONTAL GU shadows from walls); **no roof blocks a lamp**, so the hall's lamp already lights a closed room's floor through an intact concrete roof. "The light above comes in through the hole" therefore needs a VERTICAL term:
- **Above / below.** A lamp is ABOVE a roof column when its anchor level (`_anchor_level`, from `height_class`) is at or above that roof's lowest level; a room's own lamp (below its roof) is untouched.
- **Transmission, per voxel column, projected straight down.** A lamp above reaches a cell under a roof multiplied by the roof's transmission AT THAT VOXEL COLUMN: opaque material 0, intact glass a restricted share (R-D3, a per-material `light_transmission`, start ~0.35, calibrated on screen), a destroyed voxel 1. At voxel resolution (8×8 per GU) a hole draws a patch of light of its own shape on the floor under it.
- **Outdoor light** (open scenes only): a map-level `outdoor_light` {class sun / moon / overcast / ambient, intensity} acting as a lamp above every column: full on open floor, through the roof's transmission under it. Built when an open map needs it; the fixture uses the hall lamp.
- **Recompute** only the columns under a roof that changed (the consequence light already re-derives the changed cells after a blast).
- ⚠️ **It changes the look of maps that exist:** a room under an intact opaque roof stops receiving an outside lamp's light (SEG_*, PLAYGROUND, STRESS, OCCLUSION_*): its interior reads darker unless it has a lamp of its own. That is the rule R-D1 states, but it is a visible change on every roofed map.

## 1. What exists today (inventory, 2026-10-11)

- **A blast damages roof voxels:** `BlastCalculator` buckets every CEILING slab on a flooded GU (`hit_roofs`), `DetonationPlanBuilder._phase_roofs` runs the wall ring model on them (D14 falloff, `carved_side` always BOTTOM). The voxels vanish; WALK / SOOT / BURN see them.
- **Nothing else.** No debris, no falling pieces, no pile, no decal on a roof hole's rim; **a shot never damages a roof** (`AgentShotController` resolves a slice or a prop block only); **no support check** (`GlassSupport` only reads `store.pane`; a roof slab is never a pane), so a roof piece over walls that fell stays in the air (`GLASS_MASTER_PLAN` G-S1, the defect that opened the rule).
- **Glass roofs render OPAQUE** (G1: a CEILING slab is never a pane; `voxel_board.gd:882-884`) and break like any material: no shards, no rain, no craze. PLAYGROUND (`[38,2]`, 3×1) and STRESS (13×11) have glass roofs; no map has a skylight; `lift` is unused.
- **Light ignores a hole.** There is no sky / directional term in `VoxelLightField`; the "under structure" darkening (VL-D3, ×0.68) is taken from the INTACT build, so the floor under a hole stays as dark as under the roof. Only the voxels at the hole's edge brighten (`exposed_top`).
- **The reveal** (roof cutaway by slab adjacency, `OcclusionSet`) is keyed on slab objects, not voxel visibility: no tested interaction with a destroyed roof.
- **Fixtures:** OCCLUSION_ROOM (3×3 concrete roof), OCCLUSION_HALL (15×15), SEG_* (concrete), SURFACES_GALLERY; no take or POI on a roof. Roof selftests cover building and occlusion, none destruction.
