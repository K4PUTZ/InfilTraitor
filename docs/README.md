Session: `PROMPTS/RESUMO_SESSAO_2026-08-26_PERF_WAVE_AND_CONSEQUENCE.md`. **Earlier — v1.0, 2026-08-22, the spike that opened it:** Opened at the Director's instruction after the burn stall. **The one sentence: every per-cell visual property in this engine is delivered by minting a TileSet alternative and rewriting the cell** — soot rides in the alternative's `modulate.a`, the light bucket in its RGB — which is why a cosmetic effect can cost seconds and why the NEXT effect will cost the same. The plan moves per-cell state into a DATA TEXTURE the voxel shader samples by cell; Rule 8 is untouched (voxels still reach the tilemap via `set_cell()`, only their colour source changes). ⚠️ **The soot hypothesis was tested and is WRONG**, and the number is why the plan is not about soot: only **4.8%** of minted alternatives are soot-code duplicates (53 623 minted, 51 059 distinct by tile/bucket/flip) — the multiplier is (source, atlas coords) × light bucket. Soot's derivation is 146 ms of a ~4 100 ms burn. **PERF-SPIKE-01, all three gates:** cell → position is exactly AFFINE on every voxel layer (0.000000 px error out to cell (350,350), basis e1 (16,8) / e2 (-16,8) identical on all 32 layers, 200/200 inverse round-trips on real placed cells, and each level being its own TileMapLayer removes the third dimension for free); the SHADER recovers the cell per fragment via `VERTEX - local` = the quad's top-left, constant across the quad, so all three faces of a voxel resolve to ONE cell (capture: `spike_cell_index_parity_zoom.png`); and the per-cell fetch works over real art with zero alternatives (`spike_cell_index_datatex_zoom.png`) at **9.8 MB for 32 levels, 2.2 ms to rewrite a whole level, 0.1 ms for a 64×64 patch, 1.1 ms to re-upload all 32** — against the ~1 080 ms map-wide apply it replaces. **What it does NOT solve, stated up front:** the ~210 ms of map-wide DERIVATION (soot snapshot, occupancy, field build) survives untouched and is P5, deliberately last. **Two real risks:** the alternative id is currently the project's RECORD of a cell's visual state and every reader must move (the occlusion ghost store has teeth), and the flip bit cannot leave the alternative because it is geometry — alternatives go from up to 3 000 per tile to 2. The spike's shader lives at `godot/shaders/experimental/` and is swapped in at runtime, because adding a `vertex()` stage to the shipped one moved 14 pixels of 921 600 with the spike disabled. Also records a reusable harness fact: **a plain boot capture of PLAYGROUND is not deterministic (34 252 px between two identical runs) — `--fixed-fps 60` makes it 0.** **⚠️ §14 ADDED 2026-08-27 — what §12 and §13 cost the look.** Both shipped green and both broke something no gate here could see. §12's frame-cost win silently shortened the blast's choreography 4.9× (§14.1); §13's ratified 2 s light beat ran and painted almost nothing, because `play_consequence_light()` skipped every `BUCKET_UNWRITTEN` cell and the shader clamps that sentinel to full-lit — **640 of 661 cells, and they arrived at the START rather than the end**, folded invisibly into the repaint's own frame (§14.2). Both fixed in `037ea0e5`, destination provably unchanged (0 differing px against a control). **⚠️ §14.3: `INFILTRAITOR_HIDE_VOXELS` DOES NOT WORK** — walls and crates are still drawn with it set — and §12.4 used it to price the voxel layers, so that conclusion needs re-measuring. §14.4 records three numbers reported before they were checked, and the cheap guard: **diff each frame against the settled END state instead of thresholding on colour**, so static scenery cancels for free.
