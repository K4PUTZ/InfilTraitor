# INFILTRAITOR — Narrative Pipeline & Roadmap

> **Story, world-building, mission context, and player motivation.**

---

## Narrative Philosophy

- **Emergent storytelling** through gameplay (not cutscenes)
- **Player agency first** — narrative supports player choices
- **Minimal dialogue** — show, don't tell
- **Environmental clues** — pieces of a larger story

**Anti-pattern:** Narrative that contradicts gameplay (e.g., "you're undetectable" when guards easily spot you)

## Mission Briefings (Medium Priority)

### Briefing Format

Each mission includes:

| Element | Content | Status |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **Title** | Mission name | Not started |
| **Briefing** | 1–2 paragraphs context | Not started |
| **Objective** | 1–2 sentence goal | Not started |
| **Stakes** | Why does this matter? | Not started |
| **Reward** | Credits, reputation, intel | Not started |

### Example Briefing

**Mission: "Corporate Espionage"**

> The rival DynaCorp syndicate is developing a new security protocol that could block our operations for months. We need their research files before they patch the security holes. Their R&D facility is a fortress, but we've identified a maintenance window tonight. Get in, grab the data, and get out.

**Objective:** Acquire DynaCorp research files

**Stakes:** Without this intel, we lose operational advantage

**Reward:** $5000 credits, +10 reputation with Agency

## Dialogue (Very Low Priority)

### Dialogue Strategy

**Minimal dialogue approach:**
- Guard chatter is ambient (radio background)
- NPC briefings are text-based (no voice acting)
- No extended conversations
- Focus on player agency, not NPC exposition

### Guard Chatter (Ambient)

| Type | Example | Context |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Radio static** | "[Frequency interference]" | Background |
| **Guard communication** | "Sector 3 clear" | Status updates |
| **Faction jargon** | "Protocol Delta in effect" | Immersion |

### NPC Briefings

- Text-based, not voiced
- 1–2 screens max
- Establishes mission context
- Allows player to skip

## Mission Structure Framework (Medium Priority)

### Three Act Structure (Per Mission)

**Act 1: Setup**
- Briefing delivered
- Context established
- Objective clear

**Act 2: Execution**
- Player enacts plan
- Complications arise
- Decisions made

**Act 3: Resolution**
- Objective complete/failed
- Consequences shown
- Next mission teased

### Branching Outcomes (Future)

**After mission, player can:**
- Complete objective silently (best outcome)
- Complete with alarms (messy)
- Fail to complete (worst outcome)
- Discover alternative solution (bonus)

## Tone & Style

### Target Tone
- Spy thriller (not action blockbuster)
- Tense, methodical
- Consequences matter
- Failure is possible

### Anti-Patterns
- Quippy one-liners
- Over-the-top action
- Cartoon villains
- Laughing at player failure

### Visual Metaphors
- Shadows = safety
- Light = danger
- Silence = victory
- Noise = discovery

## Writing Guidelines (Future)

When adding narrative content:

1. **Clarify goal** — What does player need to know?
2. **Show, don't tell** — Use environment/actions over exposition
3. **Respect player time** — Keep text concise
4. **Avoid contradiction** — Narrative must support gameplay
5. **Build immersion** — Stay in tone
